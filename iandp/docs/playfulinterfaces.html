Playful Interfaces

process documentation

Desc
remimagine a digital tool as a physical interface
When
Oct 26
To everything saved in my phone, all my screenshots, the saved folders, and all my pinterest boards,

You have my sincerest apologies for regularly forgetting your existence, filling you with irrelevant and unused items and not really knowing the point of you. I wish I knew exactly how to use you, or what to do with everything you contain.


The modalities of how we save information on digital devices are such that they often go unused. Having limitless, shapeless, structure-less space makes it so that I do not refer back to the things I have saved. They exist without purpose, for idle browsing rather than active inspiration.


This structure means that saving replaces remembering, and the absence of context means that I often forget to use something, miss it entirely or am unsure why I saved it. Places for saving become aspirational rather than inspiration, and the infiniteness of the space removes any curation from the process.


This made me consider the physical interactions that inspired these applications to begin with.
