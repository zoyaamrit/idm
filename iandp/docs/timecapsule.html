Time Capsule

Desc
design a time capsule, looking closely at the things you live with and hold onto
When
Oct - Nov 26

Part 1

For this time capsule, I chose to concentrate my collection to jewelry. Of all the objects I own, the most discernible collections are my books and my earrings. For a capsule, I think my jewelry fits all the possible paramters: it is worn, precious, ordinary, inherited, strange, worn out, sometimes-forgotten, connected to people, place, and routine.
PEARL FLOWER
These earring have been essentially ever present, I feel like I've seen them worn for years by my mom. She still has another set very similar to this, one she wears more often that I do this. I will present this as a diptych, both earrings paralleled.
JHUMKA
Green in an unconventional colour for a Jhumka, the beads are normally white and more often contrasted with gold. Also from my mom's collection, this was one earring that my sister stole and wore nearly daily. The colour clashing with the red of her school uniform, until I kept it. Now it's seen with my most regular wear, jeans and a plain shirt, the only colour the bright green of the earring. I want to capture the sound of this.
SILVER WATCH
Bought in 2010, for a wedding anniversary in Delhi. I'm not sure which of my parents picked it out for my mom, but I've been wearing it since 10th grade. The literal passing of time, and then object.
WATCH
One of this first times I knew exactly what I wanted and looked for it with purpose.
RING
$20 in my first year, first month of college. I bought this at a flea market with a friend. I wear it almost daily, and there was no family involved with this purchase.
